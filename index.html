Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Top Tier Performance, LLC ("TTP", "we", "us"), a Michigan company, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website (app.toptier-perform.com and toptier-perform.com), the Top Tier Performance app and the TTP Consultant Portal, how we use it, who helps us run it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information: your name and email (and phone, if you give it) when you take a free check, request a free resource, apply for coaching, contact us, ask about a team program or keynote, or join a waitlist.
  • Free check and tool answers: your answers and scores from the Mental Game Scorecard, Team Check, Practice Check and the free tools, used to show and email your results.
  • Application and inquiry details: sport, level, age range, goals and anything else you choose to tell us. A parent or guardian applying for an athlete also gives their own name.
  • Payment information: handled by Stripe, RevenueCat, Apple and Google. We see what you bought and the email on the order. We never see or store your full card number.
  • App account and training data: your profile, check-ins, training activity, Notebook entries, goals and progress inside the app.
  • Notebook page scans: if you photograph a page of your paper notebook, the image is read to pull out your words, and the text is saved to your Notebook.
  • Health data: only if you choose to connect Health Connect (Android), Apple Health (iOS) or a Bluetooth heart rate sensor. With your permission the app accesses and collects steps, sleep, exercise sessions, hydration, nutrition (calories consumed), heart rate, heart rate variability and resting heart rate. See Health Data, Health Connect and Apple Health below for exactly what we collect, how we use it and who can see it.
  • Coaching records: if you work with a consultant through the Portal, your consultant keeps session notes, intake and consent forms, and session recordings or transcripts if you both agree to recording. These are visible to you, your consultant and the people you choose to share them with.
  • Booking information: the times you book, reschedule or cancel with a consultant.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the app crashes, it sends a crash report (the error, device model, operating system, app version and the screen that was open). No name, email or account id is attached.
  • Website analytics: Google Analytics counts visits and actions on this site using cookies. It does not receive your name or email.

How We Use It

  • To deliver what you asked for: check results, downloads, coaching replies and program information.
  • To run the app and the Portal, including your training plan for the day.
  • To answer applications, inquiries and booking requests.
  • To send emails about TTP programs and resources, and texts only if you ticked the box to get them. You can unsubscribe or reply STOP at any time.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To find and fix errors, keep accounts secure and improve our services.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising.

AI Features

Some features use artificial intelligence to draft text: the in-app coach's daily message, reading Notebook page scans, and, in the Portal, the Session Companion that drafts notes for a consultant to review. These requests go to our AI provider, Anthropic, which does not train its models on this data. A consultant approves every Session Companion draft before it is saved. AI output can be wrong, and it never replaces your consultant's judgment or a licensed professional.

The audio walkthroughs in our printable packs, and some audio in the app, are voiced with an AI model of Ashleigh Carpentier's own voice, reading scripts she wrote and approved.

Services We Rely On

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: data storage, sign-in, hosting and crash reporting (Crashlytics never receives your name, email or account id).
  • Anthropic: the AI features above.
  • Zoho (Mail, Campaigns and CRM): form replies, result emails, the email lists you choose to join, and our record of inquiries.
  • Stripe: payments for coaching, Portal plans and printable packs.

  • Twilio: text messages you agreed to receive.
  • Amazon Web Services (Chime): video sessions in the Portal.
  • Calendly: scheduling a call.
  • Printify and Amazon: our merchandise and books. Orders you place there follow their own privacy policies.
  • Google Analytics: website visit counts.

Health Data, Health Connect and Apple Health

The Top Tier Performance app can access and collect health data from Health Connect on Android and Apple Health on iOS. This is optional. The app works without it, and nothing is read until you turn it on in the app and grant permission on your device.

What health data we access and collect

Access is read only. The app never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health. You choose each data type separately, and we read only the types you allow:

  • Sleep: how long you slept each night.
  • Heart rate variability and resting heart rate: daily readings.
  • Steps: your daily step count.
  • Exercise: the length of your workouts.
  • Hydration and nutrition: water intake and calories consumed, if you log them.
  • Heart rate: readings for the minutes of a breathing, relaxation, meditation or focus session, from Health Connect, Apple Health, a paired watch or a Bluetooth heart rate sensor. We keep a summary of that session (start, end, lowest, highest and average), not a continuous record of your day.
  • Active calories: on iOS only.

How we use health data

  • To show your Body Signals at check-in and on your calendar: whether your sleep, heart rate variability and steps are below, within or above your own usual range.
  • To fill in your sleep hours at check-in so you do not have to type them.
  • To show your heart rate after a session, so you can see how your body responded.
  • To shape the daily message from the in-app coach and your weekly recap.

We use health data only to provide these features to you. We do not sell it. We do not use it or share it for advertising, marketing, credit, insurance or employment decisions, and we do not transfer it to data brokers.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Storage: the readings are saved to your TTP account in Google Firebase and Google Cloud, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • You: you can always see your own readings in the app.
  • Your consultant: only if you connect with a consultant and choose to share your check-ins with them. You can turn that sharing off at any time.
  • Your team: team staff never see your individual readings. A team view shows only an anonymous group summary, and only when enough athletes contribute that no one can be singled out.
  • AI coach: to write your daily message, our AI provider, Anthropic, receives your sleep duration and whether your readings are below, within or above your usual range. It does not receive your raw heart rate variability or heart rate readings, and it does not train its models on this data.

No one else receives your health data, unless the law requires us to disclose it.

Your control, retention and deletion

  • Turn any data type off, or disconnect completely, in the app under Settings, or remove the app's access in Health Connect or Apple Health on your device. The app stops reading at once.
  • We keep the readings already collected while your account is open so your trends stay intact. Ask us to delete them at any time at info@toptier-perform.com, and we will within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account deletes your health data with it.

Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Our use of Apple Health data follows Apple's HealthKit rules.

Other Health Information

Coaching records, intake forms and wearable readings can include health information. We store them in Google Cloud with encryption at rest and in transit, limit access to you, your consultant and the people you share with, and refer clinical concerns to licensed providers. TTP provides mental performance coaching, not medical or mental health treatment.

Children and Teens

  • The TTP app is for athletes 13 and older. We do not knowingly let anyone under 13 create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13.
  • A parent or guardian may apply for coaching or join a list for a child under 13. In that case the parent gives us the information and stays our contact.
  • Athletes 13 to 17 use TTP with a parent's or guardian's knowledge. When an athlete under 18 starts working with a consultant or joins a sponsored team program, we email the parent or guardian a single-use link to review and confirm consent.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their athlete's information at any time by emailing info@toptier-perform.com.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without a parent, email us and we will delete it.

Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is open, or as long as we need it to provide the service you asked for or to meet legal and professional record-keeping duties. Inactive lead and inquiry records are reviewed and removed. When you delete your app account, your account data is deleted, except records we must keep by law.

Your Choices

  • Unsubscribe: every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Reply STOP to any text.
  • Access, correction or deletion: ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at any time. You can also delete your account inside the app.
  • Wearables: disconnect a device in the app at any time.

Email info@toptier-perform.com for any of these. We respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, your state's law may give you more rights, and we honor them.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and the data tied to it at any time.

  • In the app: open Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
  • By email: write to info@toptier-perform.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete My Account". We process the request within 30 days.

Deletion removes your account information, profile, check-ins, training activity, Notebook entries, goals, progress and any health data you connected. It is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep only the records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

Security

We use encryption, access controls and account security to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us right away at info@toptier-perform.com if you think your account has been misused.

Changes to This Policy

When we make meaningful changes, we update the date at the top of this page. For major changes we also tell account holders by email or in the app.
